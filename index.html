ABOUT KIMRI

Kigumo Makomboki Recovery Initiative (KIMRI) is a community-based organisation committed to restoring hope, supporting families, and helping individuals affected by drug and substance abuse rebuild their lives.

Through community awareness, rehabilitation support, counselling, education, and family engagement, KIMRI aims to promote healthier communities and reduce the harm caused by drug and substance abuse.

Our Mission

To empower the people of Kigumo through rehabilitation support, education, counselling, and community participation, helping individuals overcome the challenges of drug and substance abuse and supporting families as they work towards recovery.

1. Rehabilitation center

Promote access to appropriate rehabilitation services and recovery support for individuals affected by drug and substance abuse.

2. Education and awareness

Educate young people, families, and the wider community about the dangers of drug abuse, prevention, and the importance of seeking help.

3. Counselling and support

Provide counselling services, support groups, and resources to help individuals and families cope with the challenges of drug and substance abuse.

4. Community Reintegration

Encourage acceptance, dignity, and community involvement to help people in recovery rebuild relationships and participate positively in society.
