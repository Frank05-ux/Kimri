WHO WE ARE

About KIMRI

The Kigumo Makomboki Recovery Initiative (KIMRI) is a community-based initiative established to address the growing challenges of alcohol, drug and substance abuse affecting individuals, families and the wider community.

Conceived through the vision of Mr. James Muhia, KIMRI brings together community members and stakeholders committed to preventing substance abuse, supporting rehabilitation and helping affected individuals reintegrate into society.

The initiative also seeks to address domestic violence, family conflicts and other related social challenges through counselling, psychosocial support and community-based interventions.

KIMRI is committed to restoring hope, rebuilding lives and strengthening families through community engagement, partnerships and sustainable recovery programmes.

Our Vision

A healthy, empowered and drug-free community where individuals realize their full potential.

Our Mission

To prevent and reduce alcohol and substance abuse through community engagement, rehabilitation services, capacity building and strategic partnerships that promote holistic well-being.

OUR OBJECTIVES

Our Core Objectives

KIMRI works toward these objectives to support prevention, recovery and stronger families across the community.

01

Prevention Programmes

Combat alcohol, drug and substance abuse through prevention programmes.

02

Accessible Rehabilitation

Establish a fully operational, accessible and affordable rehabilitation centre in Makomboki.

03

Resource Mobilization

Mobilize financial, material and human resources through partnerships with stakeholders, donors and well-wishers.

04

Family and Psychosocial Support

Address domestic violence and related social challenges through professional counselling, psychosocial support and family-centred interventions.

Strategic Activities

  • Community mobilization, forums, barazas and outreach campaigns.
  • Resource mobilization and partnerships with government institutions, NGOs and development partners.
  • Collaboration with schools, healthcare providers and local administration.
  • Counselling, rehabilitation and reintegration programmes.
  • Capacity building and training for teachers, parents, caregivers and community leaders.
